SetLog Privacy Policy

Developer: Synth Studios
Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how SetLog handles your information.

Information stored on your device

SetLog stores the information you enter in private app storage on your device. This includes exercises, workout plans and history, sets, notes, ratings, body weight, carbohydrate and caffeine entries, and app preferences. This information is used to provide workout logging, progress tracking, and the app's other features.

No developer-operated collection or tracking

SetLog does not require an account or send your workout records to Synth Studios. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services. Synth Studios does not receive or sell your locally stored workout records.

Android backups and device transfers

SetLog allows Android to back up its database and eligible preferences and to transfer them between devices. Depending on your device settings and backup provider, this may include cloud storage, such as backup through your Google account. These services are controlled by Android and the relevant provider, not by Synth Studios. You can manage backups through your device and account settings.

Exports and clipboard

If you export a backup, SetLog writes your saved records and selected settings to the location you choose using Android's file picker. That location may be on your device or with a cloud storage provider. Exported backup files are not encrypted by SetLog. Protect these files and share them only with people or services you trust. You can also choose to copy workout summaries and workout templates to your clipboard.

Notifications and optional music controls

SetLog uses notifications for workout reminders and active workouts. It can restore reminders after your device restarts. If you enable optional music controls and grant notification access, SetLog uses that access to discover active media sessions and show song titles, artists, and playback status. This information is used in memory for music controls, not saved to workout records or sent to Synth Studios. The notification listener does not read or store notification message contents. You can revoke notification access in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain in app storage until you delete them or clear the app's storage through Android settings. Clearing app storage removes local app data. It does not remove exported files, clipboard copies, or backups held by other services. Manage or delete those copies separately through the relevant location or provider. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved records.

Contacting us

If you email Synth Studios, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it as needed to handle that request. Please avoid sending sensitive workout or health information unless necessary.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
